## What

Represent several Kanmer projects through a registry of named, project-bound endpoints with observable health, controllers, workspaces, policy and sync state.

## Why

Several projects may be operated at once, but no endpoint may route a mutation to an arbitrary request-selected path.

## Approach

- Define registry entries around logical identity, source/board locations and endpoint binding.
- Surface observational cross-project status while retaining explicitly scoped per-project mutation.
- Reuse the project identity contract for every endpoint response.
