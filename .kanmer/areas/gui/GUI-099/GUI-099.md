## What
a fixed, version-independent shim at `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Kanmer\bin\kanmer-mcp.cmd` owned by the installer (created on install, updated in place on upgrade, removed on uninstall), resolving the current install via HKCU and preserving the caller's cwd (ADR-0012 discovery).

## Why
Codex registrations today embed machine-specific absolute paths; every update or machine move breaks them. Source material: archived GUI-094's research/plan (kept on its ticket).

## Verification
- [ ] shim survives an app update
- [ ] uninstall removes it
- [ ] cwd preserved
