## What

Settings' left nav ends with a bare **`?`** below Connect, where every other
entry is a named tab. Remove it. The manual does not need a door here — F1 and
Help → Manual already open it.

## Why

It reads as a broken or unlabelled tab rather than a help affordance: same
styling as the real tabs (`className="tab help-link"`), no text, no icon, sitting
in a vertical list of words. Its `title`/`aria-label` say what it does, but
nothing visible does.

The intent was good — `Settings.tsx:202-213` deep-links into the manual at the
chapter for the open tab, via `SETTINGS_HELP` (`:52-58`). But a per-tab deep
link is only worth a permanent piece of chrome if the chapters are worth landing
on, and [[DOC-007]] found that **8 of 12 manual chapters are ~100-character
stubs**. Two of the five targets `SETTINGS_HELP` points at (`stages`,
`profiles`) are stubs today. The link currently leads somewhere embarrassing.

## Approach

- Remove the button. Decide `SETTINGS_HELP` with it: the comment at `:202-204`
  claims *"the target ids are asserted to exist by manual.test.ts"*, but grepping
  `SETTINGS_HELP` in `manual.test.ts` finds **no reference** — verify that claim
  before relying on or deleting the map.
- `onOpenManual` may have no callers left afterwards; remove the prop rather than
  leaving a dead one threaded through.
- If per-tab help is wanted later, it belongs after [[DOC-007]] makes the
  chapters worth reading, and as something legible — not a bare glyph in a list
  of words.

## Verification

- [ ] Settings nav shows only named tabs.
- [ ] F1 and Help → Manual still open the manual.
- [ ] No unused props, imports, or map entries left behind; typecheck clean.
