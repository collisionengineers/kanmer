## Inherited findings from the CORE-118 independent review (2026-08-28)

The review of [[CORE-118]] PR #297 (head `924d7294`, attestation `scratch/review.md` version `1f0d9f12360713f7`, verdict pass) dispositioned six findings `deferred-to-ticket: CORE-127`. They are recorded verbatim there; carried here so planning does not have to rediscover them. Several were verified empirically by the reviewer against the built core, not merely read.

- **F-002 (minor) — globs never fire the forbidden check.** `## Do not modify` is matched as exact-set membership, so the glob idiom the repo's own plans use (`apps/gui/**`) does not forbid `apps/gui/main.ts`. Detection built on `forbiddenFiles` will therefore under-report unless the matcher understands globs.
- **F-003 (minor) — path escapes reach `allowedFiles`.** `normalisePlanPath` accepts absolute and parent-traversing paths, so `/etc/hosts` or `../other/x.ts` compile into `allowedFiles` with zero blockers. This matters most here: a detection layer that trusts `allowedFiles` would classify an edit outside the repo as authorised. Containment belongs on the compile side, but the check belongs on both.
- **F-004 (minor) — evidence pins are counted, not matched.** `requireEvidencePin` is satisfied by pin count rather than a live match; a pin naming no live document degrades to advisory `PLAN_EVIDENCE_UNKNOWN` and never blocks. Stale-evidence detection (this ticket's second acceptance bullet) cannot rely on the pin being real.
- **F-008 (minor) — read race between documents and revision.** Documents are read at `execution-packet.ts:591` and `getRevision` at `:623`, so a concurrent plan edit pairs stale plan bytes with a newer ticket revision. Any staleness comparison this ticket adds must decide which side it trusts, or read both under one snapshot.
- **F-009 (note) — `step: "next"` has no reconciliation.** It advances on checklist state alone with no reconciliation of the previous packet, which is precisely FRD-033 acceptance 4 and the reason this ticket exists.
- **F-011 (note) — vocabulary drift.** `PLAN_EVIDENCE_UNKNOWN` is emitted (`plan.ts:551`) but is absent from the finding vocabulary documented in `tool-reference.md:142-154`.

Also relevant though not deferred here: `compileStepPacket({select: 0})` throws a `TypeError` at `step-packet.ts:218` when called directly through the now-public `@kanmer/core` export — unreachable through MCP because of the zod `.int().positive()` guard, but reachable by any other consumer. And *any* `###` heading under `## Ordered steps` becomes a step, inflating `step.total`, though selection fails closed.
